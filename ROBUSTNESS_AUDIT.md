# Paper 04 revision 3 — adversarial proof review

The prior manuscript and its exact test evidence were re-read. The central results are spectrum-uniform complex-Haar convergence for fixed 0<q<2; fixed-dimensional cut and moment thresholds for real/complex Haar; and rank-one critical/stable limits. We checked the normalization, row-direction conditioning, small-index negative-moment exceptions, uniform truncation, separation of fixed-n and large-n limits, and the multiplicity-based cut bound. No counterexample or fatal inconsistency was identified in this review; this is not a proof of error absence.

New contribution: exact graph-coordinate cross-block identity and explicit gap-dependent lower and upper norm bounds, giving an independent local proof of the cut exponent. The canonical Frobenius tangent metric (half the projection embedding metric) is fixed explicitly. A tubular normal-coordinate argument yields the leading coefficient of the Frobenius cross-block small-ball probability. The derivative, normal Jacobian, compactness, disjoint strata and dominated-convergence boundary are specified. The result is NOT an exact coefficient for the entrywise maximum or MST tail, nor uniform as spectral gaps vanish. Rank-one beta endpoints and the real/complex n=2 volume normalizations independently check the scale.

Proof risks: verify the normal exponential coordinate expansion and induced product metric; each spectral off-block multiplier has real multiplicity beta*(m_a-r_a)*r_b. Min-codimension strata alone contribute to the leading power. The proof uses standard smooth tubular geometry, not new claims about the tubular theorem itself. Exact algebra checks and finite beta/volume calibrations are distinguished. The new theorem is a candidate extension; general tubular volume asymptotics are classical and an exhaustive novelty determination is not claimed.
